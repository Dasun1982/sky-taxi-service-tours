Sri Lanka Surfing

Weligama, Hiriketiya, and Arugam Bay — the three real surf destinations SKY covers, on the south and east coasts.

Overview

South coast surf schools and east coast surf points

Weligama is the south coast's main surf-school beach, Hiriketiya is known for its horseshoe-shaped surf bay nearby, and Arugam Bay is the east coast's dedicated surf town with its own established surf points.

Real destinations

Real surf destinations

Hiriketiya

Arugam Bay

East coast surf town popular with surfers, with access to nearby national parks.

Common questions

Planning your trip

Which is better for beginners, Weligama or Arugam Bay?

Weligama is generally considered more beginner-friendly with established surf schools; Arugam Bay has a bigger reputation among more experienced surfers, though both attract a range of levels.

Are south coast and east coast surf seasons the same?

No — the two coasts follow different monsoon patterns. See What Is the Best Time to Visit Sri Lanka? for the seasonal split.
