About Sinharaja

Sri Lanka's last major rainforest reserve

Sinharaja is a rainforest reserve known for birdwatching, waterfalls, and tea plantation views along its edges. It's a genuinely different kind of stop from the beach and Cultural Triangle routes most itineraries focus on.

SKY runs a real, priced one-day trip to Sinharaja — there is no dedicated taxi-service page for it yet, since it's a single-destination nature trip rather than a multi-stop transport route.

Sinharaja FAQ

Common questions

What can I see in Sinharaja in one day?

SKY's real one-day trip to Sinharaja focuses on the reserve itself — birdwatching, forest walks, and waterfalls along the trail.

Is Sinharaja good for non-birdwatchers?

Yes — the rainforest walk and waterfalls are worthwhile even without a specific interest in birdwatching, though birdwatchers get the most out of it.

Can Sinharaja be combined with a multi-day tour?

Not currently in SKY's existing round-tour packages — it's available as its own one-day trip. Ask on WhatsApp if you'd like to discuss adding it to a custom route.
