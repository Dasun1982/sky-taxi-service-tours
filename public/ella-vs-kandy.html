Which one fits your trip

Best for hiking & viewpoints

Ella — Little Adam's Peak, the Nine Arch Bridge, and tea country views are the main draw.

Best for culture & heritage

Kandy — the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake, and the Royal Botanical Gardens.

Typical trip stage

Kandy usually comes first from Colombo or the Cultural Triangle; Ella usually follows as the hill country hiking stop.

Ella vs Kandy FAQ

Ella or Kandy — which should I visit?

Ella suits travelers who want hiking, viewpoints, and the Nine Arch Bridge. Kandy suits travelers who want culture and the Temple of the Tooth. Most multi-day routes visit both.

Can I visit both Ella and Kandy?

Yes — see Ella to Kandy for the route guide, or SKY's real 3-day tour that already combines both with Sigiriya and Nuwara Eliya.
