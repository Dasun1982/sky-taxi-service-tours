One private journey, with guiding-oriented travel support

Chauffeur Guide combines private vehicle travel, driving, and a more guided experience across your destinations. It is normally best suited to journeys of 5 days or more, but you can ask SKY about another duration.

Your daily service

What the Chauffeur Guide rate covers

Up to 150 km per day is included. For longer driving days, send your itinerary and SKY will confirm the current quote.

Included in your Chauffeur Guide service

  • Private vehicle and chauffeur-guide service
  • Guide meals
  • Guide accommodation
  • Fuel
  • Highway charges
  • Parking
  • Up to 150 km/day

Not included unless arranged

  • Guest accommodation and meals
  • Attraction entrance, safari, and activity tickets
  • Train tickets, flights, and third-party services
  • Personal expenses

Your quote will set out the exact services for your route. It does not automatically cover all guest holiday costs.
